# 18.7 Tournament Level Classification

## Tournament Levels

G     Grand Slam

1000  Masters 1000
M     Masters 1000 (legacy)

500   ATP 500
250   ATP 250

C     Challenger
F     Futures / ITF

D     Davis Cup
O     Olympic Games

A     Legacy ATP category

- G
- M
- 1000
- 500
- 250
