# CORE MODEL V1

## Purpose

Identify the minimum set of variables capable of reproducing the predictive and economic performance of the full production model.

---

# Executive Summary

The investigation demonstrates that a very small subset of variables captures nearly all predictive power and the majority of economic value generated by the complete model.

A five-feature model reproduces approximately:

- 99.3% of Full Model AUC
- More than 90% of observed ROI
- Similar edge distribution
- Similar prediction ranking quality

while reducing complexity by almost 94%.

# Main Discovery

The dominant signal family is not ranking.

The dominant signal family is:

Readiness

represented by:

- delta_surface_inactivity_days
- delta_inactivity_days
- days_inactive_a
- days_inactive_b

The second strongest signal family is:

Strength

represented by:

- delta_elo

---

# Working Hypothesis

Markets appear to price historical player quality efficiently.

Markets appear less efficient at pricing:

- Recent activity
- Surface-specific activity
- Competitive readiness

The majority of edge appears when:

Strong Readiness
+
Positive Elo Profile
+
Market Undervaluation

occur simultaneously.
